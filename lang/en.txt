With no route or in other rooms: also, on a map that packs several rooms, those outside yours, as long as yours has any. Either way, except in the fixed order, the ones in your room come first and the rest follow by how many rooms away they are.

help_name_signs=Says what an invisible event with text is by what that text says, such as a bin, a shelf or a locked door, instead of a generic sign.
help_route_style=Which route is chosen when there are several. The shortest: the fewest steps and, between two equal ones, the fewest turns. Balanced: one extra step is accepted for each turn it saves. The straightest: the fewest turns, even if longer, up to a quarter more plus 4 steps; past that, the balanced one. The fewer the turns, the fewer directions to follow.
help_reach=How far, in tiles counting horizontal plus vertical, targets are found and routed to. The farther, the more targets are reached, though long routes may run into the pathfinder limit.
help_astar=How many tiles the pathfinder may examine in each search. Higher finds longer or more winding routes but takes a little longer.
lbl_path_algorithm=Route algorithm
algo_astar=A-star
algo_weighted=Weighted A-star
algo_greedy=Greedy, beeline
algo_dijkstra=Dijkstra
algo_bfs=Breadth-first, BFS
algo_dfs=Depth-first, DFS
algo_jps=Jump, JPS
algo_hpa=Hierarchical, HPA-star
algo_regions=Regions
help_algo_astar=A-star: the shortest, optimal route, searched afresh every time. Regions uses it until it knows the map.
help_algo_weighted=Weighted A-star: explores fewer tiles, a bit faster, but the route may not be the shortest.
help_algo_greedy=Greedy: heads straight for the target, ignoring distant obstacles. Quick to compute but often detours.
help_algo_dijkstra=Dijkstra: optimal like A-star but with no heuristic, so it explores more tiles. Same route, just slower.
help_algo_bfs=Breadth-first: explores level by level. Optimal in number of steps, like A-star but slower.
help_algo_dfs=Depth-first: dives deep down one path before trying others. Long, winding routes. For experimenting only.
help_algo_jps=Jump (JPS): a faster A-star that leaps along straight corridors, exploring fewer tiles. The route takes as few steps as A-star's, though among routes as short it may pick one with more turns. It only works with the preferred route set to the shortest; with the others, A-star is used.
help_algo_hpa=Hierarchical (HPA-star): splits the map into blocks and routes block by block. It is approximate: the route can be much longer than the shortest, and here it is not faster than A-star. On maps with ledges, ice or events that move the player it uses A-star.
help_algo_regions=Regions: once the pathfinder knows the whole map, it works out the route from what it learned without asking the game again: the same routes as A-star, faster, and never cut short on huge maps. Until it knows the map it uses A-star, and if A-star runs out of budget, it keeps exploring the map and gives the route. The recommended option; works well with "Explore the map in the background".
lbl_edge_relax=Lenient map edges
help_edge_relax=If an exit or floor on the map border reads as inaccessible (detected as a wall), turn this on to reach it. Experimental, off by default.
lbl_regions_background=Explore the map in the background
help_regions_background=After entering a map or when an event ends, the pathfinder explores the map a little on every frame, so routes and hidden targets answer at once on big maps too. While it lasts, games that already run at their limit may lose a little smoothness; on small maps it finishes almost at once. If you notice stutters, turn this option off.
lbl_regions_slice=Time per frame
help_regions_slice=How long each frame may spend exploring the map while the character stands still. With more time it finishes sooner; if you notice stutters, lower it. While walking it never goes over 2 milliseconds.
lbl_neighbour_cache=Keep neighboring maps
help_neighbour_cache=To tell whether the player can leave the map from a border tile, the game loads the whole neighboring map every time it is asked. With this option a copy of each neighboring map is kept, so the target list answers at once on maps connected to others. The answers are the same. It uses a little more memory; turn it off only if something seems wrong when leaving a map by its edge.
lbl_neighbours_background=Load neighboring maps in the background
help_neighbours_background=While the player stands still, the neighboring maps are loaded one by one, so the first press on each map answers at once too. Each load may be felt as a small stutter, which is why this is off by default.

lbl_guide_distance=Guide distance
help_guide_distance=How many tiles to your left or right the guide (and the jump) plays from when the step goes that way; up and down it plays where you stand, higher or lower. Closer feels more immediate; farther stops the sound from sliding to the other ear when you walk fast.
lbl_route_limit=Pathfinder limit
rlim_effort=by effort
rlim_time=by time
rlim_none=none
