lbl_path_algorithm=Route algorithm
help_path_algorithm=Changes the pathfinding algorithm, for experimenting. A-star is the optimal default; weighted A-star and greedy head straighter (odder routes); Dijkstra and breadth-first give the same route as A-star; depth-first wanders.
algo_astar=A-star
algo_weighted=Weighted A-star
algo_greedy=Greedy, beeline
algo_dijkstra=Dijkstra
algo_bfs=Breadth-first, BFS
algo_dfs=Depth-first, DFS
algo_jps=Jump, JPS
algo_hpa=Hierarchical, HPA-star
help_algo_astar=A-star: the shortest, optimal route, and fast. The recommended option.
help_algo_weighted=Weighted A-star: explores fewer tiles, a bit faster, but the route may not be the shortest.
help_algo_greedy=Greedy: heads straight for the target, ignoring distant obstacles. Quick to compute but often detours.
help_algo_dijkstra=Dijkstra: optimal like A-star but with no heuristic, so it explores more tiles. Same route, just slower.
help_algo_bfs=Breadth-first: explores level by level. Optimal in number of steps, like A-star but slower.
help_algo_dfs=Depth-first: dives deep down one path before trying others. Long, winding routes. For experimenting only.
help_algo_jps=Jump (JPS): a faster A-star that leaps along straight corridors. Same optimal route as A-star while exploring fewer tiles; falls back to A-star automatically on ice maps.
help_algo_hpa=Hierarchical (HPA-star): splits the map into blocks and routes block by block. Very fast on huge maps; the route is near-optimal, not always the shortest. Falls back to A-star automatically on ice maps.
lbl_edge_relax=Lenient map edges

lbl_route_cache=Cache maps in pathfinder
